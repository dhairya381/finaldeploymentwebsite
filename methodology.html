/ 01 FAQ & Q&A Optimization

Structure answers the way AI systems retrieve them.

Large Language Models (LLMs) rely on question-answering paradigms to fulfill search intent. If your content directly replicates the question a user types, and answers it immediately, the model is highly likely to extract and quote your exact text.

We identify high-frequency natural language questions and structure them in clean, conversational formats. This ensures prompt-retrieval systems select your page as a direct resource — rather than a competitor's.

FAQ Structure Guidelines
  • Use clear H2/H3 headers phrased as the exact user question
  • Place the direct answer in the first sentence (under 40 words)
  • Support every answer with one specific data point or statistic
  • Apply JSON-LD FAQPage schema markup to every Q&A pair
  • Group questions by topic cluster, not keyword
/ 02 Authority & Citation Optimization
What AI models prefer to cite
  • Citations from peer-reviewed databases or authoritative sources
  • Absolute numbers — "45% increase" over "significant growth"
  • Attributed expert quotes with organization and role
  • Clear entity relationships (brand → product → category)
  • Recency signals — dates, version numbers, current year references

Make your content citable by design.

Search models like Gemini and Claude do not just generate text — they cite sources to avoid hallucinations. These engines favor content that contains quantitative evidence, source links, and clear authority markers.

By formatting your landing pages with verifiable facts and specific statistics, you make it easier for LLMs to use your page as an anchor reference. We audit your content for citation-readiness and rewrite sections to maximize extractability.

/ 03 Technical Schema Integration

Help AI parsers map your brand to its context.

Traditional web crawlers catalog pages. LLM scrapers extract relational knowledge. To help them map relationships, we deploy structured data tags like JSON-LD, FAQ Schema, and Product Schema.

This helps AI parsers associate your brand name directly with target keywords, services, and statistics in their internal knowledge graphs — making you the correct answer when queries arrive.
