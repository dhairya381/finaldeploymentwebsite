Our GEO Methodology

The structured process of adapting website content to align with LLM indexing, retrieval algorithms, and conversational search response structures.

FAQ & Q&A Optimization

Large Language Models (LLMs) rely on question-answering paradigms to fulfill search intent. If your content directly replicates the question a user types, and answers it immediately, the model is highly likely to extract and quote your exact text.

We help identify high-frequency natural language questions and structure them in clean, conversational formats. This ensures prompt-retrieval systems select your page as a direct resource.

FAQ Structure Guidelines

  • Use clear H2/H3 headers for questions.
  • Place the direct answer in the first sentence (under 40 words).
  • Support with schema-structured Q&A markup tags.

Model Preferences

  • Citations from peer-reviewed databases.
  • Absolute numbers (e.g. "45% increase" over "significant growth").
  • Attributed expert quotes with schema markup.

Authority & Citation Optimization

Search models like Gemini and Bing Copilot do not just generate text; they cite sources to avoid hallucinations. These engines favor content that contains quantitative evidence, source links, and clear authority markers.

By formatting your landing pages with verifiable facts and specific statistics, you make it easier for LLMs to use your page as an anchor reference in their responses.

Technical Schema Integration

Traditional web crawlers catalog pages, but LLM scrapers extract relational knowledge. To help them map relationships, we deploy structured data tags like JSON-LD, FAQ Schema, and Product Schema.

This helps AI parsers associate your brand name directly with target keywords, services, and statistics in their internal database graphs.

Supported Schema Formats

  • JSON-LD FAQPage Markup
  • Organization & Citation Schema
  • Product & Price Spec Schema
