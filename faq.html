GEO Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the science of optimizing content for Large Language Model search engines.

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the newest evolution of search engine marketing. Instead of ranking links on search results pages, GEO structures your content so that AI search engines (like Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Copilot) read, retrieve, and cite your page as the primary source when answering users' direct prompts.

How does GEO differ from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on web-crawler ranking factors: backlink quantity, loading speed, meta tags, and exact keyword matching. GEO focuses on semantic retrieval factors: citation authority, clear data attribution, readability metrics, and question-answer alignments that make it easy for LLMs to paraphrase and link back to you.

Why are FAQs so critical for AI engine citations?

AI search models operate on natural language prompts. When a user asks a question, the model looks for direct, structured, authoritative answers to that exact question. By formatting your page with clear FAQs, you align directly with the query intent of the model, vastly increasing the chance of being quoted.

What is citation authority in GEO?

AI search engines want to justify their answers with references to avoid hallucinating. They favor sources that include specific data (statistics, study results), quotes from attributed experts, and high-quality structured links. Integrating these elements creates a strong citation signal that search models trust.

How to track ROI from AI search optimisation?

Tracking ROI from AI search optimization involves monitoring increases in branded search volume, referral traffic from AI chatbots (like ChatGPT and Perplexity), and engagement metrics on pages optimized for AI answers. Measuring brand sentiment and citation frequency in AI responses is also crucial.

What is AI search optimisation?

AI search optimization, also known as Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) or Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), is the process of structuring and formatting website content so that AI-powered search engines (like Gemini, ChatGPT, and Claude) easily retrieve, cite, and recommend your business in their conversational answers.

How to build an AI search optimisation strategy?

To build an AI search optimization strategy, start by identifying the common questions your target audience asks. Structure your content into clear Q&A formats, use robust schema markup (like JSON-LD FAQPage), back your claims with authoritative statistics, and ensure high readability so large language models can easily extract your information.

What metrics matter for AI search optimisation?

Key metrics for AI search optimization include AI share of voice (how often you are cited in AI responses), referral traffic from AI platforms, time on page for GEO-optimized content, and increases in organic brand mentions. Tracking your position in AI overviews and generative snippets is also essential.

Will GEO replace SEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) will not entirely replace SEO, but it will significantly evolve it. Traditional SEO remains necessary for standard web navigation, while GEO is critical for informational queries and conversational interactions where users expect direct, synthesized answers from AI models.

What is generative AI engine optimization?

Generative AI engine optimization focuses on tailoring digital content to rank within the responses of generative AI models. Unlike traditional SEO which ranks links, it optimizes for semantic relevance, factual accuracy, and structured data, ensuring language models select your content as a primary source.

How do I start SEO for beginners?

For beginners, starting SEO involves understanding search intent, conducting keyword research, and creating high-quality, relevant content. Ensure your website is technically sound, use descriptive title tags, and build internal links. As AI search grows, incorporating direct Q&A formats is also highly recommended.

How to do AI search content optimization?

AI search content optimization requires breaking down complex topics into easily digestible formats. Use clear headings, bullet points, and direct answers to specific questions. Support your content with verifiable data, expert quotes, and structured schema markup to provide strong citation signals.

What is the 30% rule in AI?

In the context of AI search, the 30% rule refers to the threshold of originality required for AI models to prioritize citing a source over generic knowledge. Content offering at least 30% unique insights, proprietary data, or expert analysis is significantly more likely to be referenced in generative summaries.

What will AI SEO optimization be like in 2026?

By 2026, AI SEO optimization will heavily favor conversational query resolution, zero-click answers, and multimodal search. Optimization will shift from keyword density to semantic authority, real-time data integration, and personalizing content for AI agents acting on behalf of users.

Can ChatGPT do SEO?

Yes, ChatGPT can assist with numerous SEO tasks, including keyword research, generating meta tags, outlining content, and suggesting schema markup. However, human oversight is necessary to ensure factual accuracy, strategic alignment, and compliance with search engine guidelines.

Which AI tool is best for SEO?

The best AI tool depends on the task. SurferSEO and Clearscope are excellent for content optimization, while Ahrefs and Semrush incorporate AI for advanced keyword analysis. For generative AI search visibility, specialized platforms like Optiyx are highly effective.

Which free AI is best for SEO optimization?

For free AI SEO optimization, the base versions of ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Claude are incredibly useful for brainstorming keywords, drafting meta descriptions, and structuring content. Tools like AnswerThePublic also offer limited free searches to understand user intent.
