Methodology

Discover → Observe
→ Explain → Optimize

Four stages. Systematic. Repeatable. Each cycle improves citation probability across AI engines.

01
Discover

Map the query space. Identify categories, entities, and competitor terms where AI systems form recommendations about your market.

02
Observe

Run systematic prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude. Record who is cited, mentioned, and recommended — and who is ignored.

03
Explain

Trace citations to sources. Identify what makes competitors more citable — authority gaps, content gaps, entity gaps.

04
Optimize

Translate findings into specific content changes, schema markup, and structural fixes — delivered as actionable reports, not just dashboards.
