Pioneering Generative Search Engineering

Optiyx is dedicated to researching, measuring, and mastering how Large Language Models cite web content in conversational search environments.

Our Mission

As traditional search engines transition into conversational AI assistants like Google Gemini, OpenAI ChatGPT, and Anthropic Claude, millions of businesses face an unprecedented challenge: standard keyword indexing and backlink tactics no longer guarantee visibility.

At Optiyx, our mission is to empower organizations, law firms, and content publishers with data-driven **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** methodologies. We transform unstructured web presence into authoritative, statistically supported entity graphs that AI search engines recognize, extract, and cite as definitive answers.

Our Editorial & Research Standards

Every guide, benchmark tool, and strategy published by Optiyx is produced through empirical testing across public and specialized generative search models. We test prompts against multiple LLM architectures—evaluating semantic parsing speed, citation retention rates, structured JSON-LD readability, and statistical proof weighting.

Empirical LLM Auditing

We systematically prompt leading models with thousands of commercial and informational queries to analyze citation patterns and authority weighting.

Schema & Semantic Precision

Our engineering team builds validated Schema.org entity graphs that bridge human-readable prose with machine-interpretable data structures.

Who We Serve

Optiyx serves legal practices, SaaS platforms, e-commerce brands, and digital publications seeking to secure zero-click answer blocks and citation links in conversational AI summaries.

E-E-A-T Transparency Statement

Optiyx strictly adheres to Google's Search Quality Rater Guidelines on Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (E-E-A-T). All articles undergo technical review by AI search engineers before publication.
