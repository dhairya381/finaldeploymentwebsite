The Optiyx Methodology

We sequence our optimization process into four critical phases designed to maximize your citation probability in AI search outputs.

1

Generative Engine Audit

We crawl and prompt AI models with queries related to your product category. This reveals whether your brand is mentioned, how the AI describes you, and who your competitors are in generative search answers.

2

FAQ & Q&A Structure Formatting

AI search models love structured Question-Answer pairings. We map common user inquiries into highly detailed, Schema-coded FAQ sections on your website, facilitating easy scraping and direct quotation by LLMs.

3

Quantitative Evidence & Citation Signals

AI models prefer citing claims backed by specific statistical data. We rewrite landing pages to integrate hard numbers, expert citations, and clear source markers that models recognize as high-quality reference links.

4

Schema & Technical Metadata

Deploying advanced JSON-LD structured data and semantic HTML tags. This guides crawler bots exactly to key statements, statistics, and definitions, making them highly extractable for AI summaries.
